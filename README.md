## Domain and Motivation
 
**Domain:** Food safety and dietary risk assistance for everyday consumers.
 
Rather than treating "food safety" and "allergen/nutrition awareness" as two separate problems, we frame this project around a single, coherent consumer need: *"Is this food going to hurt me?"* Safety questions address how food is handled and stored; allergen and nutrition questions address what is in the food. Both angles feed the same underlying decision a consumer is trying to make, which keeps the knowledge base focused and avoids the "too broad, inconsistent knowledge base" pitfall.

## Knowledge Base
 
We use a small, manageable set of authoritative Australian sources, primarily from **Food Standards Australia New Zealand (FSANZ)**, to keep the knowledge base coherent and credible:
 
- FSANZ food safety fact sheets
- FSANZ allergen labelling guidance
- Better Health Channel food safety pages
- FSANZ "Food allergies" fact sheet / Allergy & Anaphylaxis Australia public resources
